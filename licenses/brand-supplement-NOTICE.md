# Supplemental service artwork

These logos identify their respective services. Copyright and trademark rights
remain with their respective owners; no endorsement is implied. These images
are separate from Aeris's MIT code and the selfh.st CC-BY-4.0 collection. No
blanket open-source license is asserted for the supplemental images (SPDX:
NOASSERTION).

GoDaddy, Patelco Credit Union, GOG, DreamHost, Rocket Mortgage, Spaceship, Zoho,
Intuit, Rockstar Games, Ubisoft and ID.me artwork was obtained from the
2FA Directory contributors' repository at:
https://github.com/2factorauth/twofactorauth/tree/92d901f308ed6adc07b2415377b011c7dd544b13

The upstream README explicitly excludes images in img/ from its MIT license
and attributes them to their respective authors. The repository's license and
image policy are retained in 2fa-directory-LICENSE.md and
2fa-directory-IMAGE-POLICY.md. Its service metadata was used to identify these
logos. Image use is for identification of user-selected authentication services.

Rocket Money, tastytrade (also matched by the former name tastyworks),
SonicWall/MySonicWall and DWService artwork was obtained from the official
service websites on 2026-10-06. The exact URLs and SHA-256 checksums are recorded
in assets/brand-sources/catalog.json, alongside the pinned repository paths
for the other supplemental logos. Original files are retained there for
reproducibility. Aeris rasterized SVG artwork and optimized/resized images to
at most 128×128 RGBA PNG, preserving colors and aspect ratio. The output hashes
are in assets/brands/catalog.json. Normal builds and runtime do not fetch them.
